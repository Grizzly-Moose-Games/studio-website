Bringing Joy And Comfort To The World Through Imaginative Video Games

At Grizzly Moose Games, we are dedicated to crafting imaginative video game experiences that not only entertain but also bring happiness to players. We work tirelessly to create immersive game experiences that transport players to new worlds and challenge their perceptions of what is possible. Our games are designed with a fun-first development approach, ensuring total attention to the player experience in every aspect of our projects.

Our values

At Grizzly Moose Games, we believe that video games have the potential to positively impact people's lives, and we strive to create games that reflect this belief. We are committed to fostering a culture of inclusivity, diversity, kindness, and positivity in all aspects of our work, from the games we create to the way we operate as a company.

As a company, we pride ourselves on operating with impeccable ethical standards and integrity. We are committed to unwavering honesty and transparency in our dealings with players, employees, partners, and investors alike. We believe that building strong, lasting relationships with all stakeholders is crucial to our success as a business and as a game studio.

Join us on this journey as we continue to develop and release innovative video games with a commitment to player happiness, ethical standards, and positive impact.

Meet our team

Arya Vatan-Abadi

CEO and Engineering Team Lead

Meet Arya, a dynamic software engineer with an unwavering passion for both the world of software engineering and video games. From a young age, Arya has been immersed in the gaming realm, cultivating a profound appreciation for the artistry and intricacies that define extraordinary games. Arya strives not only to create outstanding software and games but also to contribute to a brighter, more interconnected world.

Xander de Beer

Creative Director, Audio Lead

At the age of two, Xander found himself captivated by a busking jazz group. Today, he continues to channel his profound passion for music in his first foray into the world of video game composing. Drawing inspiration from various genres, he strives to evoke deep, human emotions through his music and create immersive soundscapes that allow the player to get lost in the world of the game.

Taejoon (TJ) Park

Art Director

TJ Park has over 18 years of experience in the video game and entertainment industry as an artist. He has a deep understanding of the art production pipeline and extensive expertise in managing art teams. With hands-on skills in various art styles, he has contributed to successful projects like AdVenture Communist, AdAges, Brave Frontier, Puzzle Troopers, and more.

Cole Sweet

Technical Artist

Cole has always loved games—and now he helps make them shine. A technical artist specializing in shaders and VFX, he’s worked at Big Blue Bubble, Digital Extremes, and Carbon Counts. Passionate about the intersection of art and technology, Cole believes that fun is the heart of every great game. Also, he really loves food.

Sarin Shrestha

Software Engineer

Say hello to Sarin, a software developer in love with solving problems and using open-source technology to do it. With a knack for innovation, Sarin thrives on diving into challenges that push his problem-solving skills to new heights. His passion extends to game development, where he uses his skillset to create unique and captivating gaming experiences, making it more accessible and enjoyable for players everywhere.
