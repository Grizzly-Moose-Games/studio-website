Jelli Nook

In this relaxing crafting game, you’ll mix and match ingredients to discover all kinds of jolly little jellies. Combine up to four ingredients at a time and start cooking. Some recipes are simple. Others are more challenging. Every jelly you uncover brings you one step closer to a fully stocked shop menu featuring a total of 100 adorable Jellies.
